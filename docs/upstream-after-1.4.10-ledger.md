## What the Linux layer needed

- **16-bit pixels in compat Core Graphics.** `CGContext` takes Apple's 64-bit RGBA bitmaps (16 bits a component,
  unsigned or half floats), drawn by Skia (`kR16G16B16A16_unorm`, `kRGBA_F16`; bridge codes 2 and 3, 4 for 32-bit
  float sources). `CGImage` keeps its own depth (`nativeImage`, `dataProvider`) and hands the compat's 8-bit paths a
  rounded view (`portableImage`). Linear 32-bit floats (EXR, Radiance) are encoded to extended sRGB when drawn, as
  Core Graphics converts between spaces.
- **Core Image.** Deep sources keep their precision; `RGBA16`, `RGBAh` and `RGBAf` output are real 16-bit and float
  images; gamma and curves are no longer clipped at white; `CIMaximumComponent` and `CIMultiplyCompositing` exist; and a
  filter's own `kCIInputImageKey` in `applyingFilter`'s parameters wins over the receiver, as on the Mac.
- **ImageIO.** A deep decode entry point (`compositor_qt_imageio_deep_decode`, `compositor_imageio_register_deep`) carries
  16-bit (layout 8) and linear float (layout 16) pixels; the original decode callback keeps its 8-bit contract. 16-bit PNG and TIFF read and
  write at 16 bits; EXR goes through OpenEXR 3.5.1 (with Imath 3.2.3 and libdeflate 1.26, static: new Flatpak modules and
  `scripts/build-openexr.sh`); Radiance HDR through Qt's reader. `com.ilm.openexr-image` and `public.radiance` are in the
  UTType table and the content sniff.
- **Overrides.** `GPUCanvas` takes upstream's HDR adjustment path and extended sRGB working space; `MetalWarp` works on
  the context's own format. Generators updated for upstream's new shapes: Smudge/Liquify (finishWarp taken from tip),
  the preview renderer (`render(_:sixteenBit:hdr:)`), text format 11 (`ProjectManifest.current` 13).
- **Qt host.** Paste on the New canvas screen, bar-wide drops on the tab bar, EXR in Export As.
- **Tests.** Upstream's new `DeepLayerTests` run unchanged (the test target imports the kernels and Core Image as the
  Mac target does); `Tests/CompatTests/DeepImageCompatTests.swift` covers the compat layer itself.
